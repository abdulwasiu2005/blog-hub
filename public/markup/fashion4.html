Neutral Colors and Earth Tones in Street Fashion

Neutral colors such as beige, brown, gray, and olive have become increasingly popular in streetwear. These tones create a clean and mature aesthetic.

Earth tones are easy to mix and match, making them practical for everyday wear. They also reflect a shift toward minimalism and sustainability in fashion.

This trend proves that streetwear does not always need bright colors to stand out.
