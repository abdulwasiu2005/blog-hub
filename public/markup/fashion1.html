How Streetwear Became a Global Fashion Movement

Streetwear began as a niche style rooted in skate culture and music, but it has grown into a worldwide fashion movement. What started with graphic tees and sneakers has expanded into a diverse and influential industry.

Today’s streetwear blends comfort with self-expression. Oversized silhouettes, bold prints, and layered outfits allow individuals to express personality through clothing.

The rise of social media has also played a major role in spreading streetwear culture globally, making trends accessible to people everywhere.
