The Power of Storytelling in Human History

Storytelling has been a fundamental part of human culture long before written language existed. Through stories, people passed down knowledge, values, and traditions from one generation to the next.

Stories help humans make sense of the world by turning experiences into meaningful narratives. They shape beliefs, inspire change, and create emotional connections.

Even in the modern world, storytelling remains a powerful tool in education, entertainment, and communication.

Author: Julian Marks
