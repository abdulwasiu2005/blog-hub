Why Financial Planning Is Important for Every Stage of Life

Financial planning is not only for wealthy individuals or business owners. It is a necessary practice for anyone who wants to achieve financial goals and reduce uncertainty.

Short-term planning focuses on daily expenses and emergency funds, while long-term planning includes retirement, investments, and major life goals. Planning allows you to prepare for unexpected situations.

A clear financial plan provides direction and confidence, helping individuals make informed decisions at every stage of life.
