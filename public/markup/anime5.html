The Rise of Dark Fantasy Anime

Dark fantasy anime has grown increasingly popular in recent years. These stories blend fantasy elements with brutal realism and moral ambiguity. Unlike traditional fantasy, dark fantasy presents flawed heroes and cruel worlds. Shows like Berserk and Made in Abyss explore suffering, sacrifice, and survival. The appeal lies in its honesty. Dark fantasy does not promise happy endings; instead, it challenges viewers to confront uncomfortable truths about humanity.

Author: Aisha Tanaka
