Minimalism: Living With Less to Gain More

Minimalism is not about deprivation; it is about intentional living. By reducing excess, people can focus on what truly adds value to their lives.

Owning fewer items reduces clutter and mental stress. Minimalism also applies to schedules, commitments, and relationships, encouraging simplicity and clarity.

Living with less often leads to greater freedom, peace of mind, and appreciation for what truly matters.

Author: Felix Okoro
