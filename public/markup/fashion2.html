Oversized Fits: Why Baggy Clothing Is Trending Again

Oversized clothing has made a strong comeback in street fashion. Loose-fitting hoodies, wide-leg pants, and relaxed jackets are now seen as both stylish and comfortable.

This trend is inspired by 1990s fashion and hip-hop culture, where comfort and confidence were key. Oversized pieces also allow for creative layering and styling.

Many people are drawn to baggy clothing because it offers freedom of movement while still making a bold fashion statement.
