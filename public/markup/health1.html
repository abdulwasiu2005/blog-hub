The Importance of Staying Physically Active Every Day

Regular physical activity plays a major role in maintaining overall health. It helps improve heart function, strengthen muscles, and increase energy levels.

Staying active does not require intense workouts or gym memberships. Simple activities such as walking, stretching, or light home exercises can make a difference.

Making movement a daily habit supports long-term health and improves both physical and mental well-being.
