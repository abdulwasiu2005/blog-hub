Meet ChatGPT: An AI Designed to Help and Inform

ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence created to assist people with information, ideas, and problem-solving. It uses language patterns to generate human-like responses.

People use ChatGPT for writing, learning, brainstorming, and exploring new topics. Its goal is to make knowledge more accessible and interaction with technology more natural.

As AI continues to evolve, tools like ChatGPT highlight how humans and machines can work together productively.
